## Authority Order

List the sources that govern purpose, policy, implementation, verification, and history. State how conflicts are resolved.

## Outcome

Define the observable outcome, beneficiary, time horizon, and decision owner.

## Non-Goals

Name what the system must not optimize, automate, retain, or decide.

## Users And Tenants

Define users, tenants, accessibility needs, authorization boundaries, and prohibited audiences.

## Scope Boundaries

Define included workflows, excluded workflows, trust boundaries, data boundaries, and external dependencies.

## Side Effects And Risk

List reads, drafts, writes, external communications, financial actions, privileged actions, and their reversibility.

## Human Sovereignty

Name decisions reserved to the human, approval classes, edit/reject/defer paths, and the final baptism gate.

## Success Contract

List machine-checkable acceptance criteria, quality thresholds, simplest baseline, cost/latency limits, and evidence required.

## Stop Conditions

List missing decisions, policy failures, budget breaches, unsafe states, stale evidence, and rollback triggers that stop progression.

## Provenance

Record the seven Cuneo fields: sources read; versions/hashes/dates; sections consulted; material not used; facts extracted; model synthesis; conflicts, gaps, and limits.
