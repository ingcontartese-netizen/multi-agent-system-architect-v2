## What Was Understood

State the selected request, objective, constraints, and unknowns. Do not print unrelated conversation.

## Decision Or Plan

Present the reader-facing result with the tables, timelines, diagrams, images, and checklists justified by the content.

## Evidence And Claims

For each material claim record evidence level, source address, transformation, verifier, and unresolved uncertainty.

## Assumptions

List explicit, reversible assumptions and the decision that would replace each one.

## Risk And Stop Conditions

Separate local risk from inherited risks. Each inherited risk needs source address, owner, severity, status, and required control.

## Verification

Record content, safety, aggregation, visual, print/export, link, searchable-text, anonymous-mode, and publication checks that actually executed.

## Delta And Next Gate

Link the prior edition, initial objective, explicit delta, superseded artifacts, next check, deletion/retention policy, and next approval.

## Colophon

Attach the seven-field Cuneo manifest, renderer/version details, asset licenses, edition hash, and publication decision. Identity fields are omitted consistently when `Anonymous` is true.

## Completion Evidence

State which completion levels apply and bind each PASS, FAIL, or not-applicable decision to criteria, typed errors, receiver acknowledgement, and reproducible evidence.
